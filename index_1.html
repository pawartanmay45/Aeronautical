SEC.01 — GOVERNANCE

Executive
Committee

Four working pillars keep AESA running like a checklist — not a chain of command. Every seat below answers to one of them.

01

Role & Responsibilities

Clear ownership per seat — no overlap, no gaps. Every member knows exactly what they're accountable for, every semester.

02

Innovation & Ideas

The pipeline for new builds, competitions, and projects — from FPV frames to launch pads — before they hit the workshop floor.

03

Scheduling, logistics, faculty sign-off, and venue booking for every workshop, seminar, and competition on the calendar.

04

Inter-Committee Coordination

The link between AESA, NCC Air Wing, and other student bodies — so effort doesn't get duplicated across clubs.
